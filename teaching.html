Teaching and supervision

Ferhat Ozgur Catak teaches DAT505 Ethical Hacking (MSc), DAT250 Information and Software Security (BSc), DAT945 Secure and Robust AI Model Development (PhD), E-DAT301 Cyber Security and DAT620 Project in Computer Science at the University of Stavanger.

I teach cyber security at all levels, from secure coding for bachelor's students to ethical hacking at master's level and trustworthy AI for PhD candidates. My courses are hands-on: students attack intentionally vulnerable systems in safe lab environments, then fix them.

Supervision

I supervise PhD and MSc students in penetration testing and malware analysis, quantum machine learning and quantum security, trustworthy AI, and applied cryptography.

Prospective students

If you are interested in a PhD or MSc thesis in these areas, write to f.ozgur.catak@uis.no. Include a short CV, your transcript and a paragraph on the topic you would like to work on.
