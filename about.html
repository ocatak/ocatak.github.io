About Ferhat Ozgur Catak

Short bio

Ferhat Ozgur Catak (Ferhat Özgür Çatak) is a Professor of Cyber Security in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Stavanger (UiS), Norway. His research covers three areas of cyber security: penetration testing and malware analysis, quantum technologies for security, and trustworthy AI.

He received his PhD in Informatics from Istanbul University in 2014. From 2010 to 2019 he worked at TÜBİTAK BİLGEM in Türkiye, at the National Research Institute of Electronics and Cryptology and later as a chief researcher at the Cyber Security Institute, on cryptography, malware analysis and machine learning for security. He then worked on data privacy at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) in Gjøvik (2019–2020) and on uncertainty quantification in AI at Simula Research Laboratory in Oslo (2020–2021), before joining UiS in 2021.

He created the public Mal-API-2019 malware dataset and the QuantumUQ library for quantum machine learning, and co-authored the book Trustworthy AI: From Theory to Practice. He is an IEEE Senior Member, chairs the IEEE Communications Society Norway Chapter, and was listed in the Stanford University / Elsevier World's Top 2% Scientists list in 2024.
